Research

Questions that move across scales.

My research examines how ecological pressures, institutions, infrastructures and inequalities shape mobility and urban life. The projects below are presented as research cases rather than as a list of outputs: each shows the question, scale, method and form of evidence.

01

Doctoral Research

Intermediated Pathways of Climate Mobility examines climate mobility before displacement becomes visible, focusing on socially produced thresholds, household strategies and the intermediary configurations that shape staying, waiting, circulation and movement.

Doctoral Research West Bengal, India Qualitative / STS Remote + Field Research
Threshold zone. How are climate-mobility thresholds enacted across models, schemes and lived accounts?
Why household, why meso. At what scale are mobility decisions made in practice, and where does that diverge from the scale of governance?
Intermediary configurations. What actors, resources and knowledge practices assemble around a household's mobility decision?
Cross-ecological comparison. How do the same concepts take different empirical forms across contrasting ecological settings?

02

Urban Mobility & Infrastructure Deprivation in Four African Cities

This project examines how migration histories, length of residence, household characteristics and urban context intersect with infrastructure deprivation in low-income settlements across Accra, Dar es Salaam, Freetown and Monrovia.

Quantitative 4 Cities Household Survey Regression / Indexing

The visual story should stay quiet and analytical: use smaller figures, alternating with short interpretive text, rather than making every chart compete for attention.

03

A Diagnostic Study of Recorded Disaster-Related Internal Displacement Across Countries

Comparable disaster exposure produces very different recorded displacement counts from one country to the next. This project uses a cross-national dataset and ecological-systems framing to examine where exposure becomes recorded movement, is absorbed locally, or remains less visible in official data.

Working Paper · 2026 Cross-national Regression Clustering Diagnostic Visualization

The most useful website framing is diagnostic rather than predictive: the model marks where to look, while unusually high or low recorded displacement points toward questions of constrained mobility, undercounting and institutional context.

04

The Gap Between Climate Exposure and Subnational Spending

Fiscal capacity does not guarantee urban resilience on its own. This research examines how governance quality and spending composition shape the relationship between subnational fiscal depth, climate exposure and resilience.

Comparative Policy Subnational Finance Climate Adaptation Data Visualization

The policy question is not simply how much governments can spend, but how fiscal capacity is converted into environmental protection, social protection and other adaptation-relevant functions.

05

Anticipatory Urbanism

How Intermediaries Shape Mobility and Displacement Across Ecological Gradients asks how a household becomes “urban” before anyone leaves the village. The paper locates urbanization in preparation, debt, circulation, aspiration, waiting and institutional intermediation at the point of departure.

Working Paper · 2026 RC21 Vienna Climate Mobility Urban Theory

Ecological stress reaches households through livelihood and institutional conditions; financial, social, institutional and aspirational channels then shape whether mobility, staying or immobility becomes feasible.

06

Space, Governance & Livelihood in an Informal Settlement

This work approaches informality as a spatial and social system rather than as an absence of planning. Research in Roshanpura, Bhopal examines everyday infrastructure, livelihood space, political networks, tenure uncertainty, redevelopment pressure and cultural identity.

Urban Informality Bhopal, India Participatory Fieldwork Spatial Inquiry

The central shift is from asking where governance is absent to asking what forms of governance and negotiation are already operating.
