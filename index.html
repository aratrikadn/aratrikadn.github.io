01 — About

Research across people, institutions, cities and environmental change.

I am a PhD candidate in Public and Urban Policy at The New School. My research spans climate mobility, migration governance, urban institutions, livelihood transitions and the infrastructures through which inequality becomes experienced and governed.

I work across quantitative and interpretive traditions: using regression, multidimensional deprivation measurement, research databases and spatial analysis alongside institutional, policy and science-and-technology-studies approaches.

My doctoral work examines mobility, immobility and adaptation under ecological stress, with particular attention to institutions and anticipatory governance.

Beyond the dissertation, I work on urban inequality, quantitative migration research, city governance, protection pathways, policy systems and urban spatial life.
