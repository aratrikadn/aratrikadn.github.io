Doctoral Research

Climate mobility in the threshold zone.

How do institutions shape whether households adapt, wait, circulate, remain or move under ecological stress?

The dissertation examines climate mobility through thresholds, meso-level institutions and intermediary configurations, asking how mobility decisions are assembled before displacement becomes administratively or statistically visible.

ThresholdsHow viability and displacement are enacted across models, schemes and lived accounts.
ScaleWhy household decisions and meso-level institutions sit uneasily between individual schemes and state-level governance.
ConfigurationsHow actors, resources and knowledge practices assemble around staying, moving and waiting.
