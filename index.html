01 — About

Research at the intersection of people, place and policy.

I am a researcher working across climate mobility, migration, urban policy and livelihoods. My work asks how environmental change becomes translated into household decisions to stay, adapt, circulate or migrate — and how institutions shape those possibilities before displacement becomes visible.

This website brings together my research, publications, fieldwork, teaching and ongoing projects.
