How I think and work.

My methods shift with the question, moving between lived experience, collective inquiry, statistical patterns and spatial change.

Qualitative

Telesketching · Narrative · Visual Ethnography

I work with narrative, memory, affect and place through interviews, telesketching and visual forms of inquiry. These methods help retain uncertainty, sensory detail and relationships that are easily lost when experience is reduced to categories alone.

Conceptual synthesis Translating relational and emotional accounts into spatial concepts.
Telesketching Spoken testimony becomes an annotated spatial scene.
Visual narrative Reading care, livelihood and ecological memory together.

Participatory

Co-production · Mapping · Dialogue · Collective Interpretation

I use collaborative making, visual elicitation, mapping and dialogue to build interpretation with participants. Knowledge emerges through exchange, comparison and collective reflection rather than from a one-way extraction of information.

Collective making Drawing and visual activity as shared forms of inquiry.
Dialogue & visual elicitation Using images and conversation to develop interpretation together.

Quantitative

Regression · Clustering · Indices · Overlap · Heterogeneity

I use quantitative analysis to look for patterned relationships across cases while keeping multiplicity visible. Regression, clustering, multidimensional measures and diagnostic visualization help identify interactions, overlaps and divergent trajectories.

Multicausal overlap Showing how migration explanations intersect rather than isolating one cause.
Comparative profiling Comparing multiple dimensions without collapsing them into a single measure.

GIS &
Spatial Analysis

GIS · Remote Sensing · Raster Analysis · Environmental Change

I use GIS and remote sensing to connect social processes with environmental exposure and spatial change. Raster analysis, satellite imagery and spatial comparison make it possible to examine where hazards occur, how they change over time and what urban areas they intersect.

Temporal flood comparison Comparing water conditions across dates using satellite imagery and NDWI.
Exposure & land use Connecting flood extent with the urban and infrastructural landscape.
