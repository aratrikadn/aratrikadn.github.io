About

Research across mobility, institutions and cities.

I am a PhD candidate in Public and Urban Policy at The New School. My research sits at the intersection of climate mobility, migration governance, urban institutions, livelihoods and environmental change. I am especially interested in what happens before displacement becomes visible: the period in which households adapt, borrow, wait, circulate, prepare, remain or become unable to move.

Across projects, I move between interpretive and quantitative traditions rather than treating them as separate research identities. I use qualitative and participatory inquiry, regression and comparative modeling, spatial analysis and visual methods to ask how institutional arrangements shape unequal possibilities for mobility and urban life.
